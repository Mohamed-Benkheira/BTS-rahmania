1. WHAT IS THIS APPLICATION?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Djezzy Resource Assignment Platform is a web application that centralizes
human-resource and project-staffing operations for Djezzy. It lets the company:

  * Maintain a complete, structured directory of the organization
    (business units, departments, teams, positions, locations).
  * Keep a rich profile for every employee: personal data, hierarchy/manager,
    skills with proficiency levels, certifications, languages, availability
    (percentage + dates), and current workload.
  * Manage a catalog of skills, certifications, languages, and certifications.
  * Track projects (IT and non-IT), their priorities, budgets, dates, and the
    skill/certification/language requirements they demand.
  * Assign people and teams to projects, with a controlled approval workflow
    and full status history.
  * Report on the workforce: staffing pressure, expiring certifications,
    over-allocation, and workload vs. availability.

In short: the application replaces spreadsheets and email for deciding "who
should work on what", and it keeps an auditable record of every decision.

2. TARGET USERS AND THEIR RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application distinguishes six profiles. Each logs into the admin panel at
/admin and only sees the areas they are responsible for. (Passwords are
"password" for every demo account below.)

  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  ROLE              | RESPONSIBILITIES                        | USERS
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  super-admin       | Full access to everything; manages users | admin@djezzy.test
                    | and roles; final authority.             |
  admin             | Approves/rejects assignments; authorizes| admin@example.com
                    | employees; manages projects, skills,    |
                    | certifications, languages; manages users|
                    | and edits roles.                        |
  hr                | Maintains the employee directory and    | hr@example.com
                    | their skills/certifications/languages;  |
                    | can view but not edit projects.         |
  resource-manager  | Structures the organization (units,     | resources@example.com
                    | departments, teams, positions,          |
                    | locations); manages employees.          |
  project-manager   | Creates projects and assignments, then  | projects@example.com
                    | approves them; can view employees.      |
  employee          | Can access the panel but sees no         | employee@example.com
                    | management pages (own view reserved for |
                    | a future self-service feature).        |
  ---------------------------------------------------------------

Role / permission detail (54 permissions; counts below are direct per role):

  * super-admin     (54 perms) — every permission.
  * admin           (25 perms) — employees, skills, certifications, languages,
                    projects, assignments (create/update/delete/view) +
                    approve assignments, users (all), roles (view/update).
  * resource-manager(18 perms) — business units, departments, teams, positions,
                    locations (all CRUD) + employees (all CRUD).
  * hr              (10 perms) — employees (create/update/view), skills,
                    certifications, languages (view/update), view projects.
  * project-manager ( 9 perms) — view employees, projects + assignments
                    (create/update/view) + approve assignments.
  * employee        ( 0 perms) — panel access only.

3. FEATURE WALKTHROUGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Organization & employees
  Business Units, Departments, Teams, Positions, and Locations are managed with
  full CRUD. Departments/teams can have managers. Employees are linked to a
  department, team, and position, and can also have a manager. Each employee
  profile aggregates, via relation managers: skills (with 1-5 proficiency),
  certifications (verified/unverified), languages (with level), availability
  (percentage, start/end), and workload percentages.

3.2 Skills & certifications catalog
  Skill Categories and Skills, Certifications, and Languages are managed as
  reference data, and reused by employee profiles and by project requirements.

3.3 Projects
  Projects belong to a category (IT or non-IT), carry a priority, status, dates,
  budget, and optional requirements. Project requirements and required
  skills/certifications/languages are attached to each project so staffing needs
  are explicit.

3.4 Assignments
  Assignments link employees (individual) or teams (or a mixed mode) to a
  project with a start/end, percentage, and an approval lifecycle:
    pending -> approved -> active -> completed, with rejected/cancelled paths.
  Business rules are enforced by the AssignmentService:
    * one current assignment per employee at a time;
    * only active employees are assignable;
    * status transitions are validated.
  Every transition is recorded in assignment_status_history.

3.5 Dashboard
  A custom dashboard shows:
    * 4 stat cards with tooltips and sparklines (e.g. Active Employees);
    * 3 charts: employees by department, projects by status,
      workload vs. availability;
    * 4 tables: expiring certifications, over-allocated employees, projects
      needing staffing, and a Recent Activity audit feed.

3.6 Audit logging
  Every important transition is recorded in audit_logs: assignment lifecycle
  events, employee/project create/update/delete (old vs new values), and
  user/role/permission changes. Monitoring is possible directly on the dashboard.

3.7 Access control administration
  New user and role resources were added: create users, assign roles, toggle
  "active" access, link an account to an employee profile, and manage roles and
  their permissions.

4. DATA MODEL OVERVIEW (43 tables)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Auth:        users, password_reset_tokens, sessions, passkeys, jobs,
                 job_batches, failed_jobs, cache, cache_locks.
  * RBAC:        roles, permissions, role_has_permissions, model_has_roles,
                 model_has_permissions.
  * Organization: business_units, departments, teams, positions, locations.
  * Employees:   employees, employee_skills, employee_certifications,
                 employee_languages, employee_availabilities, employee_workloads.
  * Catalog:     skill_categories, skills, certifications, languages.
  * Projects:    project_categories, projects, project_requirements,
                 project_required_skills, project_required_certifications,
                 project_required_languages.
  * Assignments: assignments, assignment_members, assignment_teams,
                 assignment_status_history.
  * Audit:       audit_logs.
  * (Reserved for the future recommendation engine: recommendation_runs,
    recommendations, project_evaluations.)

Design highlights:
  * Soft deletes on employees, projects, and skills.
  * CHECK constraints (proficiency 1-5, percentages 0-100, rating 1-5, date order).
  * 26+ unique constraints against duplicate data.
  * Partial unique index assignments_single_current: max one approved/active
    assignment per project, while keeping history.
  * employees.user_id FK uses RESTRICT so a user account cannot be deleted while
    an employee profile references it.
  * Circular manager FKs resolved safely (managers added in a follow-up migration).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Backend: Laravel (Eloquent ORM, migrations, seeders, observers, a service
    layer for assignment rules).
  * Admin UI: Filament admin panel (15 resources, 8 widgets, 12 relation
    managers) with a custom dashboard and 4 navigation groups.
  * Auth: Laravel Fortify (login, 2FA, passkeys, email verification).
  * Authorization: Spatie Laravel Permission (6 roles, 54 permissions) plus
    policy classes (17 policies) for fine-grained access.
  * Data: PostgreSQL.
  * Frontend shell: Inertia + React starter (the admin itself is server-rendered).
  * Seeding: 6 seeders + 18 factories produce realistic demo data — 4 business
    units, 9 departments, 8 teams, 12 positions, 5 locations, 30 employees,
    40 skills, 10 certifications, 8 languages, 10 project categories, 10 projects
    (5 assigned).
